Christmas tree lights switch-on

🎄

Gather round at 4:15pm as we switch on the lights of the big Christmas tree outside the hall, kindly supplied by Wilderness Wood.

Then stay and join in with some carol singing at 4:30pm.

Christmas market

Everything takes place outside, in the style of a traditional German Christmas market. Browse the stalls, warm up with a hot drink and soak up the festive atmosphere.
